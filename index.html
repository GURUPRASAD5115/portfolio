About Me

I build things people love using

I am Rama Guru Prasad, an aspiring Frontend Developer currently pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science Engineering at Seshadri Rao Gudlavalleru Engineering College (JNTU Kakinada), with a CGPA of 8.16. I have a strong foundation in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React.js, along with programming knowledge in Python. I am passionate about creating responsive, user-friendly web applications that provide a seamless user experience. My interest lies in UI/UX design, performance optimization, and modern web development practices. I enjoy transforming ideas into real-world applications. One of my key projects, the Hostel Complaint Management System, demonstrates my ability to build full-stack applications with features like role-based authentication, complaint tracking, and multilingual support. I am continuously learning and improving my skills by working on projects, exploring new technologies, and staying updated with industry trends. My goal is to become a skilled software developer and contribute to building impactful digital solutions.
